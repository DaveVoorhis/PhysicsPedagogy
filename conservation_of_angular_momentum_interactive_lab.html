Section 4.2 & Figure 2 • Planetary Orbits (Central Forces) dL/dt = 0 (L = |r||p|sinθ Conserved)

As the planet orbits the star (Figure 2), it accelerates as it nears periapsis (|r| small, |p| large) and decelerates toward apoapsis (|r| large, |p| small), while the angle θ between ext. r and p is only 90° at the apsides. All three variables—|r|, |p|, and sin(θ)—continuously compensate to keep L = |r||p|sin(θ) strictly constant, while rotational kinetic energy Krot = L²/(2mr²) surges near periapsis.
